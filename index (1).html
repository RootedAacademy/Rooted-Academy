Why Rooted

A small school with room to notice every child.

A qualified teacher in every class

Four age groups, each with its own teacher and an assistant to help. Children are known by name from the first day.

Learning through play

Stories, songs, counting, art and plenty of time outside. The oldest children build the letters, numbers and habits they need for Grade 1.

Values at the centre

Kindness, honesty, respect and responsibility are part of every day, through stories, prayer and the way our staff treat each child.

A day at Rooted

A steady rhythm children can trust.

Every class follows a printed weekly timetable. This is the shape of a typical day.

07:00Arrival, hellos and free play
08:00Morning circle: songs, calendar and a short prayer
08:30Breakfast and hand washing
09:00Language and number activities for the class's age
10:30Outdoor play and movement
12:00Lunch, then rest or quiet time
15:00Snack, then art, music, stories and kindness time
17:30Collection and goodbyes

Exact times differ by class, and rest time is shaped to each child's needs.
